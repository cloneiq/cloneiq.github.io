Biography

刘利军，工学博士，昆明理工大学信息工程与自动化学院/人工智能产业学院副教授、硕士生导师。长期从事人工智能与医学影像交叉领域的科研与教学，研究方向聚焦机器学习与深度学习、医学大模型、智能医学成像技术、医学影像智能诊断与报告生成、医学视觉问答与临床对话及放射学知识图谱等。主持或参与国家自然科学基金、科技部及省部级科研项目10余项，牵头建设“智能医学影像综合服务实验平台”。担任EAAI、ESWA、CMPB、NN、BSPC、IF、IVC等国际期刊审稿人。以第一或通讯作者在 IEEE TMI、MedIA等国际知名期刊及 BIBM、ICIC 等重要会议发表论文20余篇（SCI/EI收录10余篇），授权发明专利10余项。

Lijun Liu received his Ph.D. degree in Engineering and is currently an Associate Professor and M.S. supervisor at the Faculty of Information Engineering and Automation / AI Industry College, Kunming University of Science and Technology. His research and teaching focus on the interdisciplinary area of artificial intelligence and medical imaging, with interests in machine learning and deep learning, medical foundation models, intelligent medical imaging technologies, AI-driven medical image diagnosis and report generation, medical visual question answering and clinical dialogue, and radiology knowledge graphs. He has led or participated in more than ten research projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, the Ministry of Science and Technology, and provincial agencies, and spearheaded the development of an Intelligent Medical Imaging Integrated Service Platform. He serves as a reviewer for leading international journals, including EAAI, ESWA, CMPB, NN, BSPC, IF, and IVC. He has published more than 20 papers as first or corresponding author in internationally recognized journals such as IEEE TMI and MedIAs, as well as major conferences including BIBM and ICIC, with over ten papers indexed by SCI/EI, and holds more than ten granted invention patents.

Research Interests

Multimodal Understanding and Generation

Unified vision-language representation and multi-task learning across classification/detection/segmentation and report generation, emphasizing alignment, structured knowledge injection, and factual consistency control.

Interactive Med-VQA and Clinical Conversational Agents

Multi-turn VQA/dialogue aligned with real workflows, with tool augmentation for controllable generation and procedural decision support.

Radiology Knowledge Graphs and Knowledge Augmentation

Building image–report–pathology–guideline knowledge graphs to enable retrieval/reasoning-enhanced diagnosis and QC, closing the loop from data to knowledge to decision.
