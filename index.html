Biography

Lijun Liu received his Ph.D. degree in Engineering and is currently an Associate Professor and M.S. supervisor at the Faculty of Information Engineering and Automation / AI Industry College, Kunming University of Science and Technology. His research and teaching focus on the interdisciplinary area of artificial intelligence and medical imaging, with interests in machine learning and deep learning, medical foundation models, intelligent medical imaging technologies, AI-driven medical image diagnosis and report generation, medical visual question answering and clinical dialogue, and radiology knowledge graphs.

He has led or participated in more than ten research projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, the Ministry of Science and Technology, and provincial agencies, and spearheaded the development of an Intelligent Medical Imaging Integrated Service Platform. He serves as a reviewer for leading international journals, including EAAI, ESWA, CMPB, NN, BSPC, IF, and IVC. He has published more than 20 papers as first or corresponding author in internationally recognized journals such as IEEE TMI, MedIA, CMPB, BSPC,JIIM and JBI, as well as major conferences including BIBM and ICIC, with over ten papers indexed by SCI/EI, and holds more than ten granted invention patents.

Research Interests

Multimodal Understanding and Report Generation

Centering on unified representation and reasoning over images–text–structured knowledge/clinical data, this line of research targets diagnostic support and workflow optimization by tackling cross-modal semantic alignment and explainable evidence-chain construction. It achieves precise understanding via fine-grained lesion localization and region-to-text grounding, and improves factuality, consistency, and completeness in knowledge-augmented report generation and conclusion summarization. With uncertainty estimation and consistency constraints to mitigate hallucinations, it establishes a deployable capability foundation for multimodal clinical agents.

Interactive Med-VQA and Clinical Conversational Agents

For real-world clinical practice, this work integrates image understanding–interactive history taking–clinical reasoning–decision recommendation into a multi-turn collaborative agent. With medical imaging as the primary thread, it fuses patient history, laboratory/examination results, and structured knowledge, and proactively asks targeted follow-up questions via intent recognition and information-gap assessment. It generates traceable conclusions through lesion region–text evidence grounding, retrieval augmentation, and uncertainty modeling, while safety constraints and evidence-consistency checks are used to mitigate hallucinations and clinical risk. Supporting iterative clarification, differential diagnosis, and follow-up planning, it outputs "answer / evidence / confidence / next steps," enabling a deployable, interpretable, and auditable platform for image-assisted decision support and clinical communication.

Radiology Knowledge Graphs and Knowledge Augmentation

Built upon a radiology knowledge graph spanning findings–anatomy–diseases–tests–staging–management/follow-up, it unifies guidelines, terminologies, and real-world data into a computable representation. Via information extraction, cross-modal evidence grounding, and RAG, it supports report generation, Med-VQA/dialogue, and differential-diagnosis reasoning with consistency checks, enabling interpretable and auditable decision support. By integrating ontology constraints, graph learning, causal/rule-based inference, and incremental quality control, it improves robustness to long-tail and distribution shift while suppressing hallucinations, forming a deployable, knowledge-driven foundation for imaging intelligence.
